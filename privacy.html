Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Match and Smash is published by Progmesion, operated by Hennadii Romanovych Mykhailenko (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of Match and Smash handles information.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your high score and your preferences for sound, music, haptics, hints, and game speed locally on your device. This information is used only to provide and remember your game experience. It is not transmitted to us.

Information we do not collect automatically

Except for information you voluntarily choose to send in a support email, the app does not collect, sell, rent, share, or transmit your personal information to us or to third parties. It does not require an account. The current version does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs, and does not send analytics or diagnostics to us.

Support email

If you voluntarily contact us at progmesion.support@gmail.com, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and message content. We use it only to respond to your request and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for support, legal, or security purposes. Your email provider processes correspondence under its own privacy policy.

When you choose to contact support from within the app, it prepares a draft email in your email app. The draft may include the app version and build number, your device’s operating-system version, and its model to help us diagnose an issue. You can review and edit this information before sending. We receive it only if you choose to send the email.

Your choices

You can change sound, music, haptics, hints, and game-speed settings at any time from the in-game Settings screen. Local game data may be removed by deleting the app, subject to your device and backup settings. Because we do not maintain user accounts or server-side game profiles, we cannot access or delete local data for you.

Third-party links

The app may offer an optional link to a music creator’s website. Opening that link is your choice. External websites are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Children’s privacy

Except for information voluntarily provided in a support email, Match and Smash does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. If we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. A future version that adds advertising, analytics, or other online services will have an updated policy before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at progmesion.support@gmail.com.
