Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Match and Smash is published by Hennadii Romanovych Mykhailenko under the brand name Progmesion (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game, view ads, or contact support.

Information stored on your device

Match and Smash stores game information locally on your device to provide and remember your game experience. This can include the current game board and progress, score and high score, rewarded continuation state, the advertising-frequency counter, and your sound, music, haptics, hints, and game-speed preferences.

This local information is not sent to our own servers. We do not create user accounts, user profiles, or cloud game saves, and we do not operate our own analytics or crash-reporting service. Deleting the app generally removes this information from the device, subject to your device and backup settings.

Advertising and information processed by Google

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. It may show an optional rewarded ad in exchange for extra moves and an interstitial ad after every third completed or abandoned game. Google and its advertising partners may process information needed to deliver, measure, improve, and protect advertising.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, ad availability, and the ad partners involved, this information may include an IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), advertising or other device identifiers, ads shown and ad interactions, app interactions such as launches or video views, and technical, performance, or diagnostic information.

Google’s handling of this information is governed by its own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and information about how Google uses data when you use sites or apps that use its services. We do not create an advertising profile of our own or combine ad information with a game account because the game has no accounts.

Consent and privacy choices

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request and record advertising consent when a consent message is required for your region. The game requests updated consent information when it starts and shows a consent form when required before requesting ads.

If Google requires a privacy-options entry point for your region, a Privacy Choices option appears in the game’s Settings screen. You can use it to review or change your advertising privacy choices. The availability and contents of Google’s privacy controls can vary by region and by the messages configured for the app.

Information you choose to send to us

If you contact us at progmesion.support@gmail.com, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and message content. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for support, legal, or security purposes.

When you choose to contact support from within the app, it prepares a draft email in your email app. The draft may include the app version and build number, your device’s operating-system version, and its model to help us diagnose an issue. You can review and edit this information before sending. We receive it only if you choose to send it. Your email provider handles correspondence under its own privacy policy.

How information is used and shared

We use local game information only to operate the game on your device. We use support emails to answer support or privacy requests. Google and its advertising partners may use ad-related information as described above to provide advertising, measurement, diagnostics, fraud prevention, and related services. We do not sell support-email information or local game data.

We may disclose information we control if required to comply with applicable law, enforce our rights, or protect the security of the game or its users. Any third party with which we share user data under our control is required to provide the same or equal protection of that data as described in this policy and as required by applicable law. Google, advertising partners, and your email provider may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or otherwise remove it through your device settings, subject to backups you control. We do not have access to that local data. Support emails are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. To request deletion of information contained in a support email, contact us at progmesion.support@gmail.com. We will address requests for information under our control, subject to any legal or security retention obligations. Google’s retention and deletion practices are governed by Google’s own policies and privacy controls.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the game, other than information a person chooses to send in a support email. Because the game uses Google advertising services, parents and guardians should review the advertising and privacy choices available on the device. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us and we will address the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game’s data practices, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. If we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request access to or deletion of information contained in a support email, contact us at progmesion.support@gmail.com. To change advertising consent where the option is available, use Settings → Privacy Choices in the game. For information processed independently by Google, use the privacy controls and contact options described in Google’s privacy policy.
